# Changelog for v1.4.3-rc.8

## Film Strips

- New: convert Hasselblad/Imacon (FFF) scanner files to standard TIFF.
- New: automatically split a whole film-strip scan into individual frame images, with reliable handling of blank or over-exposed strips.

## Fixes

- Scanner TIFF files now display correctly — and with accurate colors — in the photo gallery and editor.
