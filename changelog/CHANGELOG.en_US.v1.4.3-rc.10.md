# Changelog for v1.4.3-rc.10

## Color Correction

- New: four color-correction modes — Skin Protection (default), Balanced, Deep Correction, and Preserve Original — so you can match the look to your film and subject.
- Fix: skin tones no longer shift toward purple or magenta during automatic tone adjustment.

## Roll Mask

- New: global roll mask preset — pick a mask once, then reuse it across the entire roll.

## Interface

- New: the eyedropper/area-select hint now auto-hides when the cursor is near it, keeping the view clear.
